**Event:** Gravitational Physics Seminar Series, IIT Gandhinagar & IACS Kolkata
**Type:** Invited seminar

Seminar on a maximum-entropy conjecture for quasi-circular binary black hole mergers. The conjecture
states that the remnant is the entropy-maximizing state consistent with the Bondi mass and angular
momentum of the system. The seminar also presented numerical-relativity tests of the conjecture.
