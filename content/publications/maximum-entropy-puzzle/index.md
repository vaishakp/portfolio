**Status:** Published

**Authors:** Monica Rincón-Ramírez, Nathan K. Johnson-McDaniel, Eugenio Bianchi, Ish Gupta, Vaishak Prasad and B. S. Sathyaprakash

**Venue:** Phys. Rev. Lett. **137**, 021406 (2026)

I devised the numerical-relativity method used to test the conjecture. I also helped formulate the
conjecture in terms of the Bondi mass and angular momentum. The conjecture states that the remnant of
a quasi-circular merger is the entropy-maximizing state. If it holds, the properties of the remnant
can be described thermodynamically without solving the full field equations.

See also the companion essay, [_Do black hole mergers maximize entropy?_](../do-black-hole-mergers-maximize-entropy/),
which received an Honourable Mention in the 2026 Gravity Research Foundation Essay Competition.
